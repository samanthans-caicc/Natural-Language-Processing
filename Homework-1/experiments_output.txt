Solve the following problem step-by-step. Be brief and concise, under 50 words.
A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
Answer:
Response:
Let the cost of the ball be \( x \). Then, the bat costs \( x + 1.00 \). 

The equation is:  
\( x + (x + 1.00) = 1.10 \)  

Simplifying:  
\( 2x + 1.00 = 1.10 \)  
\( 2x = 0.10 \)  
\( x = 0.05 \)  

The ball costs $0.05
